Our Story

A garage.
A dream.
A legend.

Started in 2009 by Bangalore couple Lynn Dcosta & Nathan Harris in a humble no-frills garage, THITWC set out to offer original, affordable, quality continental food to Bangalore foodies. From a tiny 25-seater to a 150-seater institution, with outlets across Koramangala, Domlur, Kammanahalli and Hyderabad.
